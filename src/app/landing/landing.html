Boids is an artificial life program, developed by Craig Reynolds in 1986, which simulates the flocking behaviour of birds,and related group motion. (...) The name "boid" corresponds to a shortened version of "bird-oid object", which refers to a bird-like object, as well as referencing the stereotypical New York pronunciation of 'bird' Wikipedia
